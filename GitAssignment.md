## Step-by-Step Task Instructions

### Task 1: Environment & Branch Setup
1. Clone the project repository to your local system.
2. Check out your allocated group branch. If it doesn't exist, create one (`develop1`, `develop2`, `develop3`, or `develop4`).
3. Create your personal feature branch originating from your group's develop branch.

---

### Task 2: Feature Implementation & Local Commits
1. Open `script.js`.
2. Locate your assigned function.
3. Implement the body logic for your assigned function.
4. Test your function locally.
5. Commit your changes.

---

### Task 3: Cross-Group Cherry-Picking
Each intern must selectively apply a specific commit from another group's feature branch into their own feature branch.

---

### Task 4: Group Integration & Merge Conflict Resolution
1. Integrate your completed feature branch into your group's assigned `develop` branch.
